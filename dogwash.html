Frequently Asked Questions

Do the machines have a dryer and is it safe for dogs?

Yes, our machine is equipped with a blow dryer that can be switched to low-high mode and is effective for drying dogs of all coats and types.

What payment is accepted?

We accept credit and debit online payments. (NO cash).

Is the water warm?

Yes, the station is 36-39 degrees to ensure your dog has a comfortable bathing experience. Please check the temperature to make sure it's okay for them.

Yes! The equipment is safe for all ages.
